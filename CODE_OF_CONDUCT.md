# Code of conduct

Brim is a small project, and everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull
requests or reviews, is expected to be respectful.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Keep discussion about Brim and on topic.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, and no sharing of anyone's
  private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or block
anyone who does not follow this. To report a problem privately, use
**Report a vulnerability** on the Security tab, or contact the maintainer
through their GitHub profile.
